Since the APK file is approximately 85MB, it's too large for standard email. Here are the three most reliable ways to get it onto your phone:

Method 1: USB Cable (Fastest)
Connect your phone to your laptop using a USB cable.
On the phone, swipe down the notification bar and tap "USB for file transfer" (select File Transfer or MTP mode).
On your laptop, open File Explorer and go to: c:\Inspection_ReactNatvie\
Copy the standalone_inspection_v1.apk file.
Open your phone's folder in File Explorer (it will show up like a USB drive) and Paste the file into the Download folder.
On your phone, open the "Files" or "My Files" app, navigate to Downloads, and tap the APK to install.

Method 2: Google Drive / Cloud Storage (Easiest)
On your laptop, open your browser and go to drive.google.com.
Upload the standalone_inspection_v1.apk file from c:\Inspection_ReactNatvie\.
On your phone, open the Google Drive app.
Find the file and tap Download.
Once downloaded, tap the notification to install it.

Method 3: Browser Transfer (No setup)
On your laptop, go to snapdrop.net.
On your phone (connected to the same Wi-Fi), go to snapdrop.net.
You will see your phone appear on your laptop screen. Click it and select the APK file.
On your phone, tap Save when the transfer finishes.
⚠️ Important: First Time Installation
Android blocks apps not from the Play Store by default. When you tap the file to install:

A prompt will say "Install Unknown Apps".
Tap Settings.
Toggle "Allow from this source" to ON.
Go back and tap Install again.

If you have Google Drive open, that's usually the most "wireless" way to go!
